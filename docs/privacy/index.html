Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or accounts, and we don't collect personal information. Bugsy's greeting remembers that you've seen it using your browser's session storage, and your light/dark choice is saved in your browser's local storage. Neither ever leaves your device.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them (see the Google Privacy Policy).
  • Chat with Bugsy (AI): messages you type in the chat are sent through our server to Groq, which runs the AI model that writes Bugsy's replies. We do not store chat messages. Don't type personal or sensitive information into the chat.
  • YouTube videos: videos show a thumbnail first. The player only loads from youtube-nocookie.com when you click play, after which YouTube's and Google's policies apply.

Advertising

We don't show ads yet. If we add advertising (for example Google AdSense), this page will be updated first to explain how ad partners use cookies and how you can opt out.

Links

We link to GitHub repositories and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact page.
